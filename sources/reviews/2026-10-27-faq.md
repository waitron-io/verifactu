## Repository inventory and disposition

The library search covered `research_notes/`, `PROVENANCE.md`, `COMPLIANCE-AUDIT.md`, the English
and Spanish website guides, and their pre-rewrite versions at `b480da9`. E17/P2 holds the
38 scope dispositions; developer-FAQ entry 1 also discusses deadlines. `PROVENANCE.md` and
`research_notes/` contain no quotations from the changed scope page. The current
`guides/your-application.md` in both languages explicitly leaves eligibility to the caller;
the current guides no longer contain every detailed boundary statement attributed to the older
guides by E17. E17 remains a dated audit, not a claim that the rewritten guides retain its wording.

The Waitron search covered `docs/compliance/`, including `verifactu-findings.md`, `research/`,
`asesor-questions.md`, `getting-to-production.md` and `regional-language-rules.md`. Its adoption
dates agree with the current FAQ and BOE. Its Q45 dates concern B2B electronic invoicing.
Its cited record, printing, language and architecture rules come from the other FAQ pages,
the developer PDF or legislation, rather than the changed scope answer. The 17 linked pages'
date-restoration experiment preserves those September bodies and links exactly.

**No fiscal behavior change was identified by this review.** This is an assessment of the
current rules and the cited claims, not proof that every lost October edit was harmless.
No runtime library or Waitron fiscal source, fiscal fixture or existing assertion was changed.
Archive behavior has new tests: normalized text, exact binary bytes, commit-pinned repository
copies, retained older versions, date-only alerts, report paths, download/write refusals and
committed-copy checksums. Package CI supplies the unchanged protocol suites and coverage.

the original 27 October downloads. To repeat the date experiment, hash each archived FAQ text
with SHA-256 before and after the single month-name replacement and compare it with the
